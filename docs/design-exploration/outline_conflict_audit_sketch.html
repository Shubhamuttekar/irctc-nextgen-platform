Border & Outline Conflict Audit

Seat Availability Card Outline Overlap Diagnosis

Mathematical diagnosis of the conflicting top status border and orange selection outline collision on seat availability cards, with a universal remediation strategy across all cards, modals, and drawers.

Current Defect (As Shown In Screenshot)

Top Status Border Overlaps Selection Outline

When a seat card is selected, it receives an orange border and a 2px orange box-shadow ring. However, border-top: 3px solid #059669 remains active, cutting through the top outline and causing corner miter artifacts.

⚠️ TWO OUTLINES OVERLAPPING
EC ₹2420
AVAILABLE - 14
⚡ 99% High Chance
Underlying Root Causes:
1. .matrix-cell.status-green applies border-top: 3px solid var(--status-available); (emerald green/blue-green).
2. .matrix-cell.selected applies border-color: var(--irctc-saffron-500); and box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px var(--irctc-saffron-500);.
3. Because border-top overrides the top border color, the top edge has a green line while the outer box-shadow is orange. At the 10px rounded corners, the green line slices through the orange curve.
4. outline: none is missing, allowing browser focus rings to add an additional blue outline on click.
Proposed Fix (Single Clean Outline Architecture)

Unified 2px Frame & Inset Internal Status Strip

The card receives a single, unified 2px crisp orange border on all 4 sides with zero box-shadow collision. The status accent is moved to an internal inset strip (::before under overflow: hidden) with outline: none !important.

Key Architectural Guarantees:
• ✅ 100% Clean Single Outline: All 4 borders share the exact same 2px saffron boundary with zero overlapping lines.
• ✅ Inset Status Strip: Status color (Green/Amber/Rose) is rendered via an internal accent strip tucked inside the card, completely isolated from the outer boundary.
• ✅ Zero Focus Ring Clashes: Explicit outline: none !important; prevents browser blue focus rings from competing with card selection.
